# ⚡ Quickstart Guide

Get studying in under a minute with zero configuration.

---

## 🏃 3 Quick Steps

### 1. Find Your Starting Point
```bash
./learn check
```
*Answer a few targeted questions so the tutor knows what topic to focus on.*

### 2. Open Obsidian
Open the `md/` folder in Obsidian to view your study roadmap, diagrams, and flashcards.

### 3. Start Practicing
```bash
./learn chat
```
*Chat with your tutor in the terminal to practice concepts from your materials.*

---

## 📚 Adding New Subjects

Whenever you have new materials to study:
1. Put files in `material/<subject_name>/` (PDF, text, markdown).
2. Run `./learn materials` to index them.
3. Run `./learn check` to find your starting point for that subject!
